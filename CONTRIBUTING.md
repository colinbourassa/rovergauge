# Policy
This project prohibits the use of large language models (LLMs) or other generative artificial intelligence (AI) technology to generate code, documentation, or any other project artifacts. All contributions must be authored entirely by humans, and any submissions containing machine-generated content will be rejected.

# Rationale
There are numerous practical, legal, and ethical considerations that led to the adoption of this policy.

* Maintainability and accountability: Contributors must fully understand and be able to explain every line of their implementation.
* Legal uncertainty: The copyright and intellectual property status of machine-generated content remains legally ambiguous.
* Ethical sourcing: Many AI models rely on training data that was harvested without the consent of the original creators.
* Energy costs: Generative AI systems require a disproportionately large amount of energy to operate.
